A word of honesty

Fitness isn't always fun. Anyone who tells you otherwise is trying to sell you something — well, I kind of am, too.

What I've found after a decade of coaching is this: the workouts themselves rarely become enjoyable right away, or maybe even ever. Most people have to sacrifice things they'd rather be doing to show up consistently.

What I've also found is that it can be made enjoyable — not by the workout itself, but by who you do it with, and how well the program actually fits your life. That's what I try to get right.

"You can find a solid program on the internet for $47. Go do it at a low cost gym — free, free, free. But will someone be there to give you immediate feedback? Will there be somebody to keep you engaged on the days you feel... meh?"
— David, Strong by Lee

01

Effective, Efficient, Personalized Coaching

A program built around you. Someone who notices when the weight needs to go up, when your form is off, and when you've had a rough week and need a different kind of push.

02

Compassionate, Understanding Coaching

You're treated like a human being who has a life outside the gym. Your schedule, your injuries, your bad days — all of it matters, and all of it gets factored in.

About David

I'm not a gym rat.
I'm one of you.

I didn't play sports growing up. I was never the athletic one. I still don't love working out, and I'm not going to pretend otherwise.

I work out because I want to stay strong and healthy for the things that actually matter — like being able to run with my kids, throw a ball, and not get winded doing it.

I became a coach almost by accident. What I found along the way was that helping someone through something hard — and watching them come out the other side — is its own thing entirely.
